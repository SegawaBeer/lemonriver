Add a story board view for a deck: pages laid out in chapter columns on a pannable canvas. Drag a card to reorder, double-click to open the page in the editor, right-click to duplicate or delete, write speaker notes straight on a card, rename a column, or add a blank page to one. The board keeps its own column structure beside the project, so a chapter page is an ordinary card — moving it never re-groups the pages it used to head.
